Need help or found a bug? Open a support request and we’ll get back to you.

How are days counted?

Arrival and departure dates count for country and Schengen totals. Schengen combines member countries over the last 180 days. UK presence estimates midnights in the 6 April–5 April tax year. Planned and future dates are excluded.

Does the app track my location?

No. You add and edit your own trips. Nothing leaves your device.

How do I add a widget?

Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, search for Borderwise and choose Travel summary, Top countries or Counter.

How do I delete my data?

Open You → Widgets, data & more → Clear travel history, or delete the app.
